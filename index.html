WHO I AM

Hi! I'm Angie — a passionate elementary education student at Montclair State University with a love for creating classrooms where every child feels seen, supported, and excited to learn. I grew up in North Arlington, NJ and have been working since my junior year of high school, building skills in teamwork, communication, and leadership along the way.

MY TEACHING PHILOSOPHY

I believe every child walks into the classroom with something valuable to offer. My role as a teacher is to nurture curiosity, celebrate small wins, and build a community where students feel safe enough to take risks and grow. Learning should feel joyful — not just for students, but for the teacher too.
